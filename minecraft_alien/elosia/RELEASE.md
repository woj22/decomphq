**Elosia – Alien City**: the Elosia map (by Zetry) with the alien villages, farmlands and roads built in, fitted to the terrain just north of spawn.

**On your phone:** download `Elosia_AlienCity.mcworld` and tap it. Minecraft imports it as a new world called **"Elosia - Alien City"**, separate from your original.
