The service

About the SRE

The SRE is the Kingdom of Belgium's foreign intelligence service on this server. It watches threats from abroad, runs agents in the field, keeps the wanted register and issues notices on behalf of the Crown.

Statuses

  • Wanted: an active warrant; arrest on sight.
  • At large: identified but location unknown.
  • In custody: detained and awaiting judgement.
  • Cleared: warrant lifted or pardoned.

Threat levels

  • Low: minor offences, approach freely.
  • Medium: repeat offender, report before acting.
  • High: dangerous, contact an officer.
  • Extreme: enemy of the Crown, do not engage alone.

Reporting

Seen someone on the register? Message an SRE officer on the server with the case number, where you saw them and when. Rewards are paid by the Crown once a capture is confirmed.
